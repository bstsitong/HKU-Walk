# Approved prototype, adapted to native phones

Mode: Operate, with Read surfaces for the student guide.

The user has chosen the existing HTML preview. Its composition, forest-green identity, map geometry, photography and outlined icons are the authority. Native status bars and safe areas belong to the device rather than a simulated phone frame.

- App background #fbfcf8; primary #184d3e; ink #203a31; secondary #637169; lines #e6eae2; pale sage #eaf1e8; warm accent #b85238 and #fae9e1.
- Restrained 20–24 point page headings, 14–15 point body text, 12–13 point secondary copy; system CJK fonts. Content-driven heights accommodate English and larger device text.
- Map home has an overlaid heading, search and category chips, map controls on the right, a lower destination sheet, a location control and three bottom tabs. The campus map is south-up with upright labels. Pinching zooms around the fingers and dragging pans the map. More points appear as the user zooms in. The blue dot shows the phone's location with an accuracy circle.
- Search is a separate screen with quick searches, category exploration, results and an empty state. Details use the original HKU photo, entrance notes and mini map.
- Routes show origin/destination selectors and swap, a schematic route, one walking itinerary with lift steps, and a thumbnail on the right of every step.
- The student guide opens with search and topic filters above a single-column feed. Entries have a short preview, a bookmark control and an image when a published photo exists.
- Me shows a sign-in prompt and the three gated features to guests. Signed-in users see a forest-green account card, a new-submission button, and tabs for submissions, bookmarks and history, grouped by place, route and post.
- Settings contains the three-language selector, project demo options and app information. Administrators use the browser-based dashboard. Map and guide data refresh when the app opens or returns to the foreground.
- Reuse the prototype's two HKU wallpaper photos with attribution and retain the OpenStreetMap attribution in the app information.
- Native controls expose roles, labels and state; touch areas are at least 48 points. Do not reproduce desktop preview controls or hardware frames inside the phone UI.
